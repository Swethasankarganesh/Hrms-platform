## Purpose

PeopleFlow supports sustainable work by providing clear and equitable time-away practices.

## Leave types

| Leave type | Annual allowance | Approval |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Annual leave | 18 days | Manager |
| Sick leave | 10 days | Auto-approved up to 2 days |
| Personal leave | 5 days | Manager |

## Request process

1. Submit the request in **Leave management**.
2. Confirm that project coverage is documented.
3. The manager approves or declines the request.
4. Approved leave is reflected in attendance automatically.

## Support

Contact the People team for extended medical leave, parental leave, or accommodations.
